# MoveWise User Guide

Welcome to **MoveWise** — your AI-powered Health & Fitness companion. This guide walks you through every feature in the app.

---

## 🏁 First Launch

When you open MoveWise for the first time, you'll see an animated splash screen followed by the **Sign In / Sign Up** screen.

### Creating an Account
1. Tap **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your **email address** and choose a **password** (minimum 6 characters).
3. Tap **Create Account**. You'll be taken directly to the Dashboard.

### Signing In
1. Tap **Login**.
2. Enter your registered email and password.
3. Tap **Login** — your previous data and workout history will load automatically.

> **Returning users:** If you were already signed in, the app will skip the login screen and take you directly to the Dashboard.

---

## 📊 Dashboard

The **Dashboard** (Home tab) is your daily activity control center.

### Stats Cards
| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| 👟 **Steps** | Your step count for today (manual or sensor-detected) |
| 🔥 **Calories** | Total calories consumed across all logged meals |
| ⏱ **Active Min** | Sum of all workout durations logged today |
| 💪 **Reps** | Total reps counted during today's AI camera workouts |
| 🏋️ **Workouts** | Total number of workout sessions ever logged |
| 🔥 **Streak** | Your current consecutive-day active streak |

### Logging Steps Manually
Tap the **"➕ Log Steps"** button to open a dialog. Enter the number of steps to add to today's count and tap **Save**.

### 7-Day Step Chart
The line chart at the bottom shows your step count for the past 7 days. The chart animates on load and uses a gradient fill for a premium look.

### AI Insight
Below the chart you'll see a personalized **AI Insight** message generated from your current step count and active minutes — it updates automatically as your data changes.

### Recent Workouts
The list at the bottom shows your **3 most recent workout sessions** with exercise type, duration, and rep count. Tap **"View All Recommendations"** to see workout suggestions.

## 🏋️ AI Workout Camera

The **Workout** tab uses your phone's camera to track your exercise in real time.

### Starting a Session
1. Tap the **Workout** tab (barbell icon in the bottom nav).
2. Grant **Camera permission** when prompted.
3. Tap **▶ Resume** to begin tracking. The timer starts counting.

### AI Exercise Detection
- Stand **2–3 meters** from your phone so your full body (or upper body for arm exercises) is visible.
- The AI will automatically detect which exercise you're doing and display it in the top chip (e.g., `🏋️ Squats`).
- Supported exercises: **Squats, Push-ups, Bicep Curls, Overhead Press, Lunges, Jumping Jacks, Lateral Raises, Planks, General Workout**.

### Rep Counting
- Reps are counted automatically by tracking joint angles through the full range of motion.
- The rep counter updates instantly in the large number display.
- Your **AI Coach** speaks encouraging feedback at reps 5, 10, and multiples of 10, and for perfect form.

### Form Score
- A color-coded **Form Score** card appears during exercises:
  - 🟢 **Green (80–100%)** — excellent form, keep it up!
  - 🟡 **Amber (50–79%)** — good, but try to go deeper.
  - 🔴 **Red (< 50%)** — adjust your range of motion.

### Plank Mode
When a plank is detected, the timer switches to a **hold timer** that announces your hold time every 10 seconds via voice.

### Controls
| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| **⏸ Pause / ▶ Resume** | Pause and resume tracking and the session timer |
| **🔄 Reset Reps** | Reset the rep counter to 0 (keeps the timer running) |
| **🔀 Flip Camera** | Switch between front and back camera mid-session |
| **✅ Finish Workout** | Open the save dialog |

### Saving a Workout
Tap **Finish Workout** to see a summary (auto-detected exercise type, duration, reps). Options:
- **Save & Finish** — saves with the auto-detected type and returns to Dashboard.
- **Edit type** — lets you manually change the exercise name before saving.
- **Keep Going** — dismiss the dialog and continue the session.

## 🥗 Nutrition Tracker

The **Nutrition** tab tracks your daily food intake and macronutrients.

### Calorie Overview
At the top you'll see:
- **Calories Remaining** — based on a 2,500 kcal daily goal.
- **Consumed vs. Burned** — consumed from meals; burned estimated from active minutes (8 kcal/min).
- A circular progress ring shows your level of the goal.

### Macro Bars
Below the calorie ring, three progress bars show today's totals vs. daily targets:
- 🥩 **Protein** — goal: 150 g
- 🌾 **Carbs** — goal: 300 g
- 🧈 **Fat** — goal: 70 g

### Water Tracker
- Current intake shown as `X / 2400 ml`.
- Tap **"+ Add Glass"** to log 250 ml of water.

### Logging Meals

**Option 1 — Manual Entry:**
1. Tap **"Log Meal"**.
2. Fill in: Name, Calories, Protein (g), Carbs (g), Fat (g), and Meal Type (Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner/Snack — pre-filled based on time of day).
3. Tap **Save**.

**Option 2 — Scan Meal (Camera):**
1. Tap **"Scan Meal"**.
2. Point your camera at your food. The AI will attempt to recognize the item in real time.
3. Tap **"📷 Capture"** when you're ready. The app searches for nutrition data online.
4. Review and confirm (or adjust) the auto-filled data in the Log Meal dialog.

**Option 3 — Scan Barcode:**
1. Tap **"Scan Barcode"**.
2. Align the product barcode within the camera frame.
3. The app auto-detects the barcode and looks up the product in the Open Food Facts database.
4. Review and confirm the nutrition data.

> **Tip:** If the scanner cannot find data online, you can always fill in the numbers manually in the confirm dialog.

## 💬 AI Coach Chat

The **Chat** tab gives you a direct line to your personal AI fitness coach.

### Sending Messages
1. Tap the **Chat** tab (message bubble icon).
2. Type your question or message in the text box.
3. Tap the **Send** button (paper plane icon).
4. A `...` typing indicator appears while the AI is thinking, then the response appears.

### What to Ask
- Workout advice: *"How many rest days should I take per week?"*
- Nutrition guidance: *"What should I eat after a leg day?"*
- Progress questions: *"I've done 3,000 steps today — how can I hit 10,000?"*
- Goal setting: *"Help me create a 4-week plan to lose 5 kg."*

### Personalizing Your Coach
Tap the **🔧 Wrench icon** in the chat header to open the **Coach Personalization** screen.

| Setting | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Coach Name** | The name your AI uses to introduce itself | "Alex", "Coach Sam" |
| **Tone** | Communication style | "Motivational", "Calm", "Direct" |
| **Focus Area** | Primary goal to keep advice targeted | "Weight Loss", "Muscle Gain", "Endurance" |

Tap **Save** — your preferences are stored and injected into every new conversation.

## 📈 Progress

The **Progress** tab helps you visualize long-term trends.

### Weight Chart
- Tap **"➕ Log Weight"** and enter your current weight in kg.
- The line chart updates to show your weight trend over all logged entries.

### Activity Breakdown
- The pie chart shows the proportion of each workout type (Squats, Push-ups, etc.) across your entire history.

### Badges
- Tap **"View Badges"** to open the **Badges Gallery** — a collection of achievement badges with unlock criteria.

## 🏅 Badges

The **Badges** screen shows all achievements you can earn:
- Badges are awarded for milestones like first workout, step count goals, streak lengths, and more.
- Locked badges appear greyed out; unlocked ones are highlighted.

|---|---|

| **My Badges** | Shortcut to the Badges gallery |

## ❓ Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|---|---|
| **AI Chat not responding / "API Key missing"** | Ensure you have added `gemini.api.key=YOUR_KEY` to your `local.properties` file in the project root. |
| **Camera not working** | Go to Android Settings → Apps → MoveWise → Permissions → Enable Camera. |
| **Nutrition scan shows "Could not find nutrition"** | Enter nutrition details manually in the dialog that appears, or try the barcode scanner. |
| **Steps not updating automatically** | Some devices do not have a hardware step counter. Use the manual "Log Steps" button on the Dashboard. |
| **Data not syncing across devices** | Ensure you are connected to the internet. Firebase syncs automatically when connectivity is restored. |
